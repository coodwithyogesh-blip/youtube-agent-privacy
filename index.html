Privacy Policy — YouTube AI Manager

Last updated: November 22, 2026

YouTube AI Manager is a tool that helps its operator process videos and thumbnails submitted through Telegram, generate video metadata, and upload videos to a connected YouTube channel.

Information we process

  • Telegram messages, video files, thumbnail images, and related information you submit to the bot.
  • Google account and YouTube channel authorization information, including OAuth tokens, needed to perform actions you request such as uploading videos and setting thumbnails.
  • Information used to generate video titles, descriptions, tags, and other metadata.
  • Basic technical logs needed to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the service.

How information is used

Submitted media and information are used to run the requested workflow, generate metadata, upload content to the YouTube channel you select, set thumbnails, respond to requests, and maintain the service's security and reliability.

Third-party services

The service relies on third-party providers, including Telegram, Google/YouTube, Google Gemini (where enabled for metadata generation), and its hosting provider. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and retention

Media files may be temporarily stored on the service while a job is being processed and are intended to be cleaned up after processing. Authorization tokens and limited operational data may be retained to support connected-account functionality. Logs may be retained for troubleshooting and security. Actual retention depends on the service's configuration and hosting environment.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information and access to connected accounts. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not send sensitive or unrelated personal information to the bot.

Your choices and deletion requests

You may stop using the service or disconnect its access from your Google Account's security settings. To request deletion of information held by the service, contact the developer using the email below and identify the relevant account or data. Requests may be subject to necessary security checks.

Children

This service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The updated date at the top of this page will indicate the latest revision.
